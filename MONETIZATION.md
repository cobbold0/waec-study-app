# WAEC Study App — Monetization Strategy

## Primary Model

The initial product should be free to use and monetized primarily through advertising.

The goal is to build useful traffic first.

Revenue should come from scale rather than frustrating students into paying.

## Advertising

Potential provider:

- Google AdSense

Do not assume approval or credentials exist.

If implementation requires an owner action, document it in `NEXT_STEPS.md`.

## Where Ads Can Appear

Good locations:

- Homepage
- Public subject pages
- Study guides
- Practice-question informational pages
- Results/summary screens where appropriate
- Content/resource pages

Avoid intrusive ads inside the active question-answer interaction.

## Where Ads Should NOT Appear

Do not place ads:

- Over answer options
- Between every question
- Directly beside buttons in a misleading way
- Where they can be mistaken for content
- In a way that causes accidental clicks
- In a way that blocks explanations
- During critical timed interactions

The student should always understand what is an advertisement and what is educational content.

## Free Experience

Free users should be able to:

- Browse subjects
- Browse topics
- Practice questions
- See explanations
- Track basic progress
- Complete study sessions

Do not add artificial restrictions just to force monetization.

## Future Premium

Possible premium features:

- **Premium Question Banks** — More extensive practice sets.
- **Ad-Free** — Remove advertisements.
- **AI Tutor** — Explain concepts and mistakes interactively.
- **Mock Exams** — Advanced timed examinations.
- **Advanced Analytics** — Detailed topic-level performance.
- **Personalized Study Plans** — Study recommendations based on performance.

Do not implement these until the free product is useful.

## Affiliate Revenue

Potential future affiliate categories:

- Educational courses
- Books
- Study materials
- Laptops/tablets
- Educational services

Affiliate relationships must be disclosed appropriately.

## Sponsorships

Future possibilities:

- Educational brands
- Universities
- Training institutions
- Scholarship organizations
- EdTech companies

Sponsored content must be clearly identified.

## Analytics

Measure:

- Visitors
- Subject views
- Study starts
- Questions answered
- Sessions completed
- Returning students
- Ad performance

Avoid collecting unnecessary personal information.

## Monetization Principle

Build:

```text
Search traffic
      ↓
Useful educational content
      ↓
Subject/topic page
      ↓
Practice
      ↓
Returning student
      ↓
Advertising
      ↓
Future premium products
```

Do not sacrifice retention for short-term ad revenue.
